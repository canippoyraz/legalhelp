# Business Associate Agreement

1. <span class="header_2">Business Associate Obligations</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Obligations and Restrictions.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> may not use or disclose PHI other than as described in this BAA, as permitted under the Privacy Rule, or as otherwise required by applicable law.
    2. <span class="header_3">Permitted Uses and Disclosures.</span>  Except as otherwise permitted or required in this BAA, <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> may only use or disclose PHI as reasonably necessary to provide the Services or as otherwise required by applicable law.
    3. <span class="header_3">Safeguards.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> will implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of PHI that it receives, creates, maintains, or transmits on behalf of <span class="keyterms_link">Company</span>.
    4. <span class="header_3">Subcontractors.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> must ensure that each Subcontractor executes a binding, written agreement requiring the Subcontractor to protect PHI under terms substantially similar to and no less stringent than this BAA.
    5. <span class="header_3">Individual Requests.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> will take reasonable efforts to support <span class="keyterms_link">Company</span> in completing requests related to individuals' rights under HIPAA in a timely manner, but in no event will <span class="keyterms_link">Provider's</span> response take more than ten business days.

2. <span class="header_2">Company Obligations</span>
    1. <span class="keyterms_link">Company</span> will notify <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> of any changes to how it uses or discloses PHI to the extent the changes impact how <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> uses or discloses PHI under the BAA.
    2. <span class="keyterms_link">Company</span> will only use and disclose PHI to <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> in accordance with its obligations under HIPAA and applicable law.

3. <span class="header_2">Breach Notification</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Breach Reporting.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> will report to <span class="keyterms_link">Company</span> within the <span class="keyterms_link">Breach Notification Period</span> each use or disclosure of PHI not permitted under this BAA.
    2. <span class="header_3">Security Incident Reimbursement.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> will reimburse <span class="keyterms_link">Company</span> for costs reasonably associated with a Security Incident caused by <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> or one of its Subcontractors.

4. <span class="header_2">Term & Termination</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Term.</span>  This BAA will start on the <span class="keyterms_link">BAA Effective Date</span> and will continue until the Agreement ends or expires.
    2. <span class="header_3">Termination.</span>  Either party may terminate this BAA if the other party fails to cure a material breach within 30 days after receiving notice of the breach.
    3. <span class="header_3">Effect of Termination.</span>  Upon termination, <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> will either return or destroy, at <span class="keyterms_link">Company's</span> discretion, all PHI maintained by <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span>, its agents, or its Subcontractors.

5. <span class="header_2">Definitions</span>
    - **"HIPAA"** means the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 and the rules and regulations thereunder.
    - **"PHI"** or **"Protected Health Information"** has the meaning given to it under HIPAA.
    - **"Privacy Rule"** means the federal privacy regulations issued pursuant to HIPAA, codified at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164.
    - **"Security Rule"** means the federal security regulations issued pursuant to HIPAA, codified at 45 CFR Parts 160 and 164.
    - **"Subcontractor"** means a third party to whom Provider provides PHI under this BAA.
