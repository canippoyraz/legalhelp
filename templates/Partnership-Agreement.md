# Partnership Agreement

1. <span class="header_2">Cooperation</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Obligations.</span>  Each party will perform its <span class="businessterms_link">Obligations</span> as detailed in the Cover Page.
    2. <span class="header_3">Feedback.</span>  Each party may, but is not required to, give Feedback to the other party. The party receiving Feedback may use it freely without any restriction or obligation.

2. <span class="header_2">Payment & Taxes</span>
    1. If the <span class="businessterms_link">Obligations</span> include payment of Fees, the paying party is responsible for all duties, taxes, and levies that apply to Fees, including sales, use, VAT, GST, or withholding.

3. <span class="header_2">Trademark License</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Trademark License.</span>  Licensor grants to Licensee a non-exclusive, non-transferrable, non-sublicensable, revocable, royalty-free, limited right and license in the <span class="businessterms_link">Territory</span> to use the Licensor's Brand Elements solely as necessary for Licensee to perform its <span class="businessterms_link">Obligations</span>.
    2. <span class="header_3">Reservation of Rights.</span>  Except for the limited license in Section 3.1, neither party transfers any rights in any of its products, data, or any other intellectual property.

4. <span class="header_2">Privacy</span>
    1. If the parties have a <span class="keyterms_link">DPA</span>, each party will comply with its obligations in the DPA.

5. <span class="header_2">Term & Termination</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Term.</span>  This Agreement starts on the <span class="keyterms_link">Effective Date</span> and continues until the <span class="businessterms_link">End Date</span>.
    2. <span class="header_3">Termination.</span>  Either party may terminate the Agreement immediately for material uncured breach (30 days notice), or upon notice for insolvency or dissolution.
    3. <span class="header_3">Effect of Termination.</span>  Upon termination, all trademark rights revert to Licensor and each Recipient will return or destroy Discloser's Confidential Information.

6. <span class="header_2">Representations & Warranties</span>
    1. Each party represents and warrants it has the legal power and authority to enter into this Agreement, is validly existing, will comply with Applicable Laws, and that its Brand Elements do not infringe any third party's intellectual property rights.

7. <span class="header_2">Disclaimer of Warranties</span>
    1. Except for the warranties in Section 6, each party disclaims all other warranties, whether express or implied, including the implied warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, and title.

8. <span class="header_2">Limitation of Liability</span>
    1. **Each party's total cumulative liability will not exceed the General Cap Amount. Neither party will be liable for lost profits, consequential, special, indirect, exemplary, punitive, or incidental damages.**

9. <span class="header_2">Indemnification</span>
    1. <span class="keyterms_link">Company</span> will indemnify <span class="keyterms_link">Partner</span> against Company Covered Claims. <span class="keyterms_link">Partner</span> will indemnify <span class="keyterms_link">Company</span> against Partner Covered Claims.

10. <span class="header_2">Confidentiality</span>
    1. Recipient will only use Discloser's Confidential Information to fulfill its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement and will not disclose it to anyone else. Recipient will protect Confidential Information using at least a reasonable standard of care.

11. <span class="header_2">General Terms</span>
    1. This Agreement is governed by the <span class="keyterms_link">Governing Law</span>. Disputes will be brought in the <span class="keyterms_link">Chosen Courts</span>. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter.

12. <span class="header_2">Definitions</span>
    - **"Brand Elements"** means a party's trademarks, service marks, names, and logos.
    - **"Confidential Information"** means information identified as confidential or that should be reasonably understood as confidential.
    - **"Feedback"** means suggestions, feedback, or comments about the other party's products or services.
    - **"Standard Terms"** means these Common Paper Partnership Standard Terms [Version 1.0](https://commonpaper.com/standards/partnership-agreement/1.0).
