# Professional Services Agreement

1. <span class="header_2">Services</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Providing Services.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> will perform the Services as detailed in an applicable SOW. <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> will comply with <span class="keyterms_link">Customer Policies</span>, if any.
    2. <span class="header_3">Cooperation.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Customer</span> will reasonably cooperate with <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> to allow the performance of Services. <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> will provide its own equipment and tools to perform the Services.
    3. <span class="header_3">Change Orders.</span>  Either party may propose amendments to any SOW by entering a Change Order. A Change Order will not be binding until both parties agree in writing.
    4. <span class="header_3">Acceptance.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Customer</span> will be deemed to have approved a Deliverable if it does not reject it within the <span class="keyterms_link">Rejection Period</span>. If rejected, <span class="keyterms_link">Customer</span> must provide written detail about why the Deliverable did not meet SOW requirements.
    5. <span class="header_3">Subcontractors.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> may use Subcontractors to perform the Services only with <span class="keyterms_link">Customer's</span> prior permission. <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> is responsible for all acts and omissions of its Subcontractors.

2. <span class="header_2">Intellectual Property</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Deliverables.</span>  Except for Pre-Existing Materials and Third-Party Materials, <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> assigns all right, title, and interest in the Deliverables to <span class="keyterms_link">Customer</span> at the Time of Assignment.
    2. <span class="header_3">Pre-Existing Materials.</span>  To the extent <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> incorporates Pre-Existing Materials into Deliverables, <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> grants <span class="keyterms_link">Customer</span> a non-exclusive, non-transferrable, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide license to use Pre-Existing Materials only as necessary to use the Deliverables.
    3. <span class="header_3">Feedback and Usage Data.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Customer</span> may, but is not required to, give <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> Feedback. <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> may use all Feedback freely without any restriction or obligation.

3. <span class="header_2">Payment & Taxes</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Fees and Invoices.</span>  Unless the SOW specifies otherwise, all Fees are in U.S. Dollars and are exclusive of taxes. <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> will send invoices for Fees as described in the SOW.
    2. <span class="header_3">Payment.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Customer</span> will pay the Fees within the Payment Period.

4. <span class="header_2">Term & Termination</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Term.</span>  This Agreement will start on the <span class="keyterms_link">Effective Date</span> and continue until 12 months have elapsed since the end of the latest SOW Term end date.
    2. <span class="header_3">Termination.</span>  Either party may terminate for material uncured breach (30 days notice), insolvency, or dissolution. Either party may also terminate an SOW if a Force Majeure Event prevents performance for 30 or more consecutive days.
    3. <span class="header_3">Effect of Termination.</span>  Upon termination, Provider will no longer provide Services, and each Recipient will return or destroy Discloser's Confidential Information.

5. <span class="header_2">Representations & Warranties</span>
    1. <span class="header_3">Mutual.</span>  Each party represents and warrants it has the legal power and authority to enter into this Agreement, is validly existing, and will comply with Applicable Laws.
    2. <span class="header_3">From Provider.</span>  <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> represents and warrants it will perform the Services in a timely, competent, and professional manner, and that the Deliverables will conform to the requirements in the SOW.

6. <span class="header_2">Disclaimer of Warranties</span>
    1. Except for the warranties in Section 5, each party **disclaims all other warranties, whether express or implied, including the implied warranties of merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, title, and non-infringement.**

7. <span class="header_2">Limitation of Liability</span>
    1. **Each party's total cumulative liability will not exceed the General Cap Amount. Neither party will be liable for lost profits, consequential, special, indirect, exemplary, punitive, or incidental damages.**

8. <span class="header_2">Indemnification</span>
    1. <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> will indemnify <span class="keyterms_link">Customer</span> against Provider Covered Claims. <span class="keyterms_link">Customer</span> will indemnify <span class="keyterms_link">Provider</span> against Customer Covered Claims.

9. <span class="header_2">Insurance</span>
    1. During the term and for six months after, each party will carry commercial insurance policies with coverage limits that meet the relevant Insurance Minimums required in the SOW, if any.

10. <span class="header_2">Confidentiality</span>
    1. Recipient will only use Discloser's Confidential Information to fulfill its obligations or exercise its rights under this Agreement and will not disclose it to anyone else. Recipient will protect Confidential Information using at least a reasonable standard of care.

11. <span class="header_2">General Terms</span>
    1. This Agreement is governed by the <span class="keyterms_link">Governing Law</span>. Disputes will be brought in the <span class="keyterms_link">Chosen Courts</span>. This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties on its subject matter.

12. <span class="header_2">Definitions</span>
    - **"Change Order"** means a document that identifies the SOW being changed, describes the changes, and is approved by both parties.
    - **"Deliverables"** means work product to be created by Provider as described in the SOW.
    - **"Pre-Existing Materials"** means information, tools, materials, or intellectual property that Provider owned or developed before or independent of the Agreement.
    - **"SOW"** means a statement of work describing the Services, Deliverables, Fees, and other details.
    - **"Subcontractors"** means other people or companies engaged by Provider to perform Services.
